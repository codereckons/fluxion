Background  {#background}
=========================

What **FLUXION** computes is a derivative, and it computes it by arithmetic rather than by
approximation. These pages follow that from the definition of a derivative to the code that returns
one, in order:

  + @subpage flx_derivation, the derivative of a function, then the recurrence that carries one
    through an execution;
  + @subpage flx_methods, the four ways of obtaining one and what each requires;
  + @subpage flx_algebra, the nilpotent units, the components they index, and the types that
    carry them;
  + @subpage flx_seeding, how a derivative is asked for and where the answer lands;
  + @subpage flx_examples, from a first derivative to a gradient at several points at once.

The @subpage flx_glossary collects the numerical and algorithmic terms they use.
