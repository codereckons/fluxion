Background  {#background}
=========================

**FLUXION** computes derivatives by arithmetic, without approximation. These pages cover, in order,
the definition of a derivative and the code that returns one:

  + @subpage flx_derivation, the derivative of a function, then the recurrence that propagates one
      through an execution;
  + @subpage flx_methods, the five ways of obtaining one and what each requires;
  + @subpage flx_algebra, the nilpotent units, the components they index, and the types that
    carry them;
  + @subpage flx_seeding, how the arguments are seeded and where each derivative is stored;
  + @subpage flx_examples, from a first derivative to a gradient at several points at once.

The @subpage flx_glossary collects the numerical and algorithmic terms the other pages use.
