{\bfseries{FLUXION}} computes derivatives by arithmetic, without approximation. These pages cover, in order, the definition of a derivative and the code that returns one:\+


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item \doxylink{flx_derivation}{Differentiating a Program}, the derivative of a function, then the recurrence that propagates one through an execution;
\item \doxylink{flx_methods}{Obtaining a Derivative}, the five ways of obtaining one and what each requires;
\item \doxylink{flx_algebra}{Hyperdual Algebra}, the nilpotent units, the components they index, and the types that carry them;
\item \doxylink{flx_seeding}{Seeding and Reading}, how the arguments are seeded and where each derivative is stored;
\item \doxylink{flx_examples}{Examples}, from a first derivative to a gradient at several points at once.
\end{DoxyItemize}
