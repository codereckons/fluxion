What {\bfseries{FLUXION}} computes is a derivative, and it computes it by arithmetic rather than by approximation. These pages follow that from the definition of a derivative to the code that returns one, in order:\+


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item \doxylink{flx_derivation}{Differentiating a Program}, the derivative of a function, then the recurrence that carries one through an execution;
\item \doxylink{flx_methods}{Obtaining a Derivative}, the four ways of obtaining one and what each requires;
\item \doxylink{flx_algebra}{Hyperdual Algebra}, the nilpotent units, the components they index, and the types that carry them;
\item \doxylink{flx_seeding}{Seeding and Reading}, how a derivative is asked for and where the answer lands;
\item \doxylink{flx_examples}{Examples}, from a first derivative to a gradient at several points at once.
\end{DoxyItemize}
